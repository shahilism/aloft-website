Our journey

Founded on the need for better HVAC execution.

As buildings become more complex, cooling solutions need to be evaluated as part of the wider building infrastructure—not as standalone equipment decisions.

Established in 2017.

Established in 2017, ALOFT set out to bring a more structured, engineering-focused approach to cooling projects. Our work centres on connecting technical planning, installation execution and maintenance support to address the practical needs of each building.

Engineering standard on every site:

01

Load calculation first

Every scheme begins with precise heat-load modeling, solar gain calculation, and occupancy profiling before equipment is selected.

02

Architectural coordination

3D duct routing, acoustic isolation, and diffuser placement coordinated strictly with interior design and ceiling heights.

03

Documented commissioning

Formal airflow CFM balancing, static pressure logging, and thermal validation prior to commercial handover.

Commercial HVAC Execution

Rigorous lifting, structural coordination, and plant room positioning.
