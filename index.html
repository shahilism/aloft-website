Our process

From project brief to reliable operation.

A structured approach helps align the cooling system with your building's requirements, project scope and operating needs.

  1. 01

    Understand the requirements

    We assess the building type, usage, cooling needs, project stage and technical constraints.

  2. 02

    Plan the solution

    We evaluate appropriate HVAC configurations, equipment and installation requirements for the agreed scope.

  3. 03

    Install and commission

    We execute the agreed installation scope and carry out applicable testing and commissioning activities.

  4. 04

    Maintain the system

    Where contracted, maintenance and service support help keep equipment in suitable operating condition.
